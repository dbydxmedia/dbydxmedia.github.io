About BYOB India

A book club with a difference — Bring & talk about your favourite books!

"A book club where you don't have to read the same book as everyone else."

Founded with a simple premise: traditional book clubs force everyone to read the same title on a deadline. BYOB (Bring Your Own Book) flips this concept upside down.

We gather to share genuine enthusiasm about whatever book has caught our imagination recently. You leave every meetup with a curated list of authentic recommendations!

Bring Your Own Book (BYOB) India is one of India's most popular and unconventional book club networks. Established in 2015, it reimagined the traditional book club structure to create an inclusive, low-pressure space for bibliophiles.

Our Core Pillars

Inclusivity

Open to all genres, languages, and types of readers.

No Spoilers

Thoughtful discussions respecting fellow readers' journeys.

Community First

Friendships forged over shared literary passion.

Origin and Founding

The Founder

Nidhi Srivastava conceptualized the club while living in the Delhi NCR region, seeking a flexible way for readers to bond.

The Inspiration

Traditional book clubs assign a mandatory "book of the month." Inspired by "Bring Your Own Booze," Srivastava swapped alcohol for literature so members bring whatever they wish to share.

Initial Launch

The first official meeting took place at Srivastava's home in 2015, featuring discussions around various titles including Stephen Chbosky’s The Perks of Being a Wallflower.

How the Format Works

Unlike conventional reading groups, BYOB operates on a flexible, non-judgmental framework:

No Mandated Reading List

Members are not forced to read a specific book. Everyone brings a book of their choice—fiction, non-fiction, poetry, graphic novels, or academic literature.

Show-and-Tell Style Discussions

During a meetup, each participant gets a few minutes to give a brief review or summary of their book, explain why they picked it, and share key takeaways.

Q&A and Debates

Following each presentation, the group opens up for interactive discussions, questions, and spirited debates on the themes raised.

Book Swaps and Lending

Members frequently lend or exchange books with one another. The club tracks borrowed titles to ensure books are safely returned to their owners.

No Cover Charges

Meetups are free of cost and open to all age groups, typically taking place in public parks, cozy cafes, community centers, or member homes.

Special Formats & Themes

While standard meetups are open to any genre, BYOB chapters frequently host specialized sessions:

Themed Meets

Focus sessions around specific genres or topics, such as translation literature, non-fiction, queer literature, graphic novels, or poetry.

Author Sessions

Interactive interactions where authors are invited to speak about their work, followed by broader discussions on related themes.

Screenings & Adaptations

Gathering to watch film adaptations of popular books, followed by a comparison between the source text and the movie.

Online "E-Meets"

Virtual Zoom discussions introduced to maintain community engagement across different geographies.

Expansion and Chapter Network

What began as a localized gathering in Delhi quickly grew into a nationwide movement through word-of-mouth and social media engagement:

Major Chapters

Active chapters operate across major Indian metros, including Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Surat, and Kochi.

Sister Chapter Model

Local chapters are organized by local volunteer administrators who adapt the core BYOB format to fit their city's cultural setting while maintaining the non-judgmental, open-access philosophy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about BYOB India

Do I have to read a specific book before coming?

Not at all! You simply bring whatever book you are currently reading or any book you'd like to share.

Is there a membership fee to join?

No, BYOB meetups are completely free to attend. Cafe meetups might require purchasing your own food/beverage.

What genres or book formats are allowed?

All genres are welcome! Fiction, non-fiction, memoirs, graphic novels, poetry, e-readers (Kindles), and physical books are all great.

How can I start a BYOB chapter in my city?

Reach out through our Contact page or City Chapters section, and our team will guide you on organizing community meetups.
