About the Creator

Where spirituality meets suitcases, yoga mats & bold outfits

Sahithi is a solo female traveler, yoga practitioner, and fashion lover based between Hyderabad and Vizag. Her content sits at the intersection of ancient wisdom and modern life — proving that you don't have to choose between being grounded and being glamorous.

Her audience — Indian women aged 22–38 — see themselves in her. They're stuck between who they are and who they want to become. She gives them permission to be all of it.
